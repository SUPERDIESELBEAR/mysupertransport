# Set staff birthdays from the Staff Directory

Today a birthday can only be set by the person themselves, in their own Edit Profile. This adds the same Month + Day birthday to the Staff Directory panel, so you can fill in birthdays for anyone on the team.

## What you'll see

- Open Staff Directory, click a staff member, and there's a new **Birthday** row next to Phone and Email.
- It shows the saved birthday (for example "March 14") or "No birthday on file".
- Click the pencil, pick a Month and a Day, save. A Clear option removes it.
- Day choices adapt to the month (28/29/30/31), same as the self-service form.
- Once saved, that person's birthday starts appearing in the celebration popup other staff already see, with the same Send button for the email and in-app note.
- Every change is written to the activity log with your name, exactly like phone and name edits.
