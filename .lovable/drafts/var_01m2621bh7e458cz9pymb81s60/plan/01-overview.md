# Why the offboarding count climbs on its own

You did nothing wrong, and nothing was actually done to those drivers. Opening the offboarding screen is what moved the number.

Confirmed in the saved records, not guessed. When the offboarding screen opens, it looks at the driver and decides which steps have no work at all — no fuel card assigned, no plates, no equipment sheet, no active agreement. It marks each of those "nothing to do" steps as handled and **saves them immediately**, before you touch anything. The ribbon on the driver's page counts every saved step, whether it was done by a person or filled in automatically. So 3 became 5 purely from opening and closing the screen.

The records show this plainly for Willie: two rows — fuel card and plates — were written at 11:25 this morning with the reasons "No fuel cards assigned to this driver" and "No MO plates assigned to this driver". No person marked them; the screen did.
