# Telling staff about new features

## What already exists

"What's New" under Admin is already a working announcement board. The moment anyone with access posts one, every staff member gets a bell notification and an email — there is no review step, and drafts don't exist. Drivers are never included. The Staff Help assistant also reads past announcements, so it can answer questions about them.

What it cannot do today: nobody has to see it, and nobody approves it. There is no draft state, no owner sign-off, no unread marker, no pop-up, no record of who read what, and an announcement can't point to the screen it's about.

## What I recommend

Keep one place for announcements. Add a review step in front of it, and make what gets published unmissable:

**1. Draft → review → published.** Anyone on management can write an announcement; it saves as a draft and sends nothing. Marcus sees it in a Pending Approval queue with three choices: **Approve & send**, **Deny** (with a reason, goes back to the writer), or **Archive** (shelved, never sent, kept for the record). Nothing reaches staff until approved. Marcus can also write and approve in one step.

**2. The owner picks the audience.** Before approving, Marcus sets who it goes to — all staff, or any mix of management, onboarding, and dispatch — and whether it needs a "Got it" acknowledgment. Drivers can never be selected.

**3. A pop-up on next sign-in.** When a staff member in the audience opens the dashboard with anything unread, one panel shows it, newest first, with "Got it" to clear. Once per announcement per person, never over a form in progress.

**4. "Take me there."** Each announcement can carry the exact screen it describes, with a button that opens it — so "where do I find this" answers itself.

**5. An unread count on the sidebar** beside What's New until the list is clear.

**6. Who has seen it.** Each published announcement shows a read list, so you know whether the change landed before assuming it did.

**7. Categories** — new feature, change, fix, reminder — so the list reads like a history instead of a pile.

Email stays as it is: staff only, through each person's Staff & Admin notification setting.
