# Telling staff about new features

## What already exists

"What's New" under Admin is already a working announcement board. When you post one, every staff member gets a bell notification and an email; drivers are never included. The Staff Help assistant also reads past announcements, so it can answer questions about them.

What it cannot do today: nobody has to see it. There is no unread marker, no pop-up, no record of who read what, and an announcement can't point to the screen it's about. So a change gets posted, a few people notice the bell, and the rest find out by being confused.

## What I recommend

Keep one place for announcements — do not add a second channel. Make the existing one unmissable, actionable, and measurable:

1. **A pop-up on next sign-in.** When a staff member opens the dashboard and has unread announcements, a single panel shows them, newest first, with "Got it" to clear. It appears once per announcement per person, never repeats, and never interrupts a form in progress.
2. **"Take me there."** Each announcement can carry the exact screen it's about. The pop-up and the What's New page show a button that opens that screen directly, so "where do I find it" answers itself.
3. **An unread dot on the sidebar.** A gold count on "What's New" until they've read everything.
4. **Who has seen it.** The composer shows a read list per announcement, so you know whether the change landed before you assume it did.
5. **Choose the audience and the weight.** Post to all staff or only certain roles (management, onboarding, dispatch). Mark important ones as needing a "Got it" — those keep reappearing until acknowledged. Ordinary ones clear on first view.
6. **Categories.** New feature, change, fix, reminder — so the list reads like a history rather than a pile.

Email stays exactly as it is: it already goes to staff only, through the Staff & Admin notification setting each person controls.
