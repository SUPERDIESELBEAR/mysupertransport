# Roadside Stop Log

## Is a roadside stop the same as a roadside inspection?

No. Today the app tracks the annual periodic inspection (Vehicle Hub) and has an upload slot where a driver can send in a roadside inspection report. There is no record of the stops themselves.

A roadside stop is the event: the driver was pulled in. It may end as a full DOT inspection with a report number and level, or as a traffic stop with a warning or citation and no inspection at all. This log covers both, and an entry can be marked as an inspection when one happened.

## What gets recorded

Every stop entry captures:

- Date and time, state and location (scale house, mile marker, city)
- Reason for the stop (random, weigh station, moving violation, equipment, logs, other)
- Type: DOT inspection or traffic stop
- Outcome: clean, violations noted, out of service, warning, citation
- Truck/unit, and the load being hauled if there was one
- For inspections: report number, level (I-VI), inspector and agency, CVSA sticker issued
- Violations: code, description, and whether it put the driver or vehicle out of service
- Attachments: the inspection report, citation, or photos

## Who can add one

Either side, no approval step. The driver files it from their phone right after the stop; the office can add or edit an entry in the Driver Hub. Edits are attributed so it is always clear who changed what.

## Where it shows

- Driver Hub: a Roadside Stops section on the driver's profile with the stop history, a count of stops and out-of-service events in the last 12 months, and an Add Stop button.
- Vehicle Hub: the same stops listed under the truck they involved.
- Operator portal: the driver sees their own history, read-only, plus a Report a Stop button.
