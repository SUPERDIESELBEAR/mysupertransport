# One place to offboard a driver and their truck

Today offboarding is really two flows that don't know about each other. The **Deactivation & Delease Wizard** (opened from the Deactivate button on a driver) walks ten steps. But the **lease termination** also has its own separate button, its own safety confirmation, and its own "send to insurance" action — and the copy created inside the wizard never reaches insurance. Equipment return exists twice, in two different forms. Plate release, fuel card, and equipment can also be done from their own hubs, with no awareness of an offboarding in progress.

This plan makes the wizard the single, complete path: start it, finish every step inside it, and nothing is left sitting in another hub.

## What changes for staff

**One start, one finish.** The separate lease-termination button on the driver profile goes away as a starting point. The safety confirmation that protects it — typing the driver's name, and the warning when the driver still looks actively dispatched with an offer to park them instead — moves into the wizard, where it belongs.

**The truck leaves with the driver by default.** The unit question stays, but it opens on "the truck leaves with the driver" since that is the normal case here. Choosing that retires the unit cleanly: plate released, equipment returned, lease ended. The other two answers stay available for the exception.

**Insurance gets the document without a second trip.** The Appendix C created in the wizard is signed, previewed in full, and sent to insurance right there, with the send recorded on the step.

**Ending the lease actually ends it.** Voiding the agreement marks it void with a reason and keeps the record, instead of deleting it. Same rule the terminations list already follows.

**Turning off the login actually turns it off.** Today the wizard asks whether to keep the login and only writes the answer to the audit trail. It will disable sign-in when staff choose to, and say plainly what happens either way.

**Equipment return is one thing.** The wizard shows the assignment sheet, the return instructions, and the physical receipt confirmation together, so there is nothing left to confirm elsewhere.

**Pick it up where you left off.** Progress is already saved per driver. The driver's profile gets a clear "Offboarding in progress — 6 of 10 done" banner that resumes at the first unfinished step, and each step shows if it was completed outside the wizard so it never asks twice.

Owner and management run it. Nobody else sees the start button.
