# Make a deactivated vehicle unmistakable at a glance

Your instinct was right, and the search actually uncovered a real defect underneath it.

## What the check found

Both plate `05KT2W` results are genuine: Matthew Clovis on unit 193 is active, Edward Williams on unit 197 is off the roster. Same plate, because the plate moved from one truck to the other — that part is correct.

But Edward's card shows **no "Deactivated" mark at all**. There is already a small "Deactivated" chip and an orange "these units are off the roster" banner built for this exact situation — neither appeared. The reason: the card decides "is this off the roster?" from a *deactivation date* field, and 77 of the 94 deactivated drivers have no date recorded. Only 17 of them would ever show the chip. So on searches like yours, a deactivated driver currently looks identical to an active one.

That is the fix worth making first — the watermark on top of it.
