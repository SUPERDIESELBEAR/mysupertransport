# Alexander White's September 9 inspection is attached — the binder just can't see it

## What is actually stored

His September 9 certificate is on the truck record and the binder row for Periodic DOT Inspections already points at that exact file. The binder row also carries the right inspection date, 9/9/2026.

What the row is missing is the **viewing link**. A binder document is saved twice over: once as the file's location in storage, and once as a ready-made link to it. Uploads made from the Vehicle Hub save only the location — no link. The sync then copies both across, so the binder ends up with a real file location and a blank link.

Every badge and button on that binder row is decided from the **link**, not the location. Blank link, so the row reads "No file", the eye is hidden, and the button says Upload instead of Replace — even though the document is right there.

This is confirmed on live data, not inferred: exactly two binder rows across the whole fleet are in this state today, both Periodic DOT Inspections, both from Vehicle Hub uploads made this week. Alexander White is one; a second driver's row from this afternoon is the other. Every other document type gets its link written at upload time, which is why nothing else looks broken.
