# Clear up drivers sharing the same plate

A new **Duplicate Plates** screen in Management lists every licence plate that appears on more than one driver, shows the drivers side by side, and lets you correct the wrong one — with a permanent record of what the plate used to be.

## What the data shows today

There are 17 plates in use on more than one driver:

- 12 where one driver is off the roster (for example 05KT2W — Matthew Clovis, unit 193, active; Edward Williams, unit 197, deactivated)
- 2 where both drivers are still active (72KU6D on units 268 and 268 — Jonathan Grant and Steven Moore; DD4761 across units 187 and 185)
- 3 where all the drivers involved are off the roster
- One plate, 53KU7B, sits on four different drivers

There is no plate history anywhere today. Plate changes on a driver's truck record are overwritten in place, so this plan adds that record as part of the work.
