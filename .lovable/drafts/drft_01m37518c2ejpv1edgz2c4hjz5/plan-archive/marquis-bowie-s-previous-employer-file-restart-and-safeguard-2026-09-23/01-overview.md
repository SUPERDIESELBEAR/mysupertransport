# Marquis Bowie's previous-employer file: restart and safeguards

## What is and isn't possible

His original file cannot be undeleted. Nothing of it survives in the system: no tracking events, no reply record, no deletion record. The only trace is one outbound email to the Schneider contact on 5 Aug 2026.

What can be done is start the checks again from his application, which still lists all three employers, and close the gap that let the deletion pass unnoticed.

## Part 1 — Rebuild his three checks

Create a fresh previous-employer request for each employer on his application:

- Schneider (MaddoxB@schneider.com)
- Focus HR (support@focushr.net)
- Falcon Jet (hortondassault@falconjet.com)

Each is created ready to send, not sent, so you review them first on his application page and press send when you're happy. Employer names, contacts and employment dates come straight from his application, so nothing is typed in by hand. His deadline date is recalculated from the new send, and the stale 4 Sep deadline goes away.

## Part 2 — Who did what, and nothing lost again

Three changes, all behind the scenes:

1. Every create, send, follow-up and delete of a previous-employer request is written to the activity log with the staff member's name and the employer involved.
2. Deleting a request no longer erases it. It is marked as withdrawn, with who did it and when, and it drops out of the normal lists. Its history stays.
3. An application whose status says previous-employer checks are in progress while no request exists behind it is flagged for staff — the exact condition on his record, and on William Westbrook's, that went unseen for seven weeks.
