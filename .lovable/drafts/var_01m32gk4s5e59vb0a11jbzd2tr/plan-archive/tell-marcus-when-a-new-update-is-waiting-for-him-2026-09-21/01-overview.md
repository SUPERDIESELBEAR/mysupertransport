# Tell Marcus when a new update is waiting for him

## Where things stand today

The announcement pipeline is already built and live: every build that changes something staff can see writes a ready-made announcement — plain-language title and body, what changed, where to find it, a "Take me there" link, audience and type — straight into your **Pending Your Approval** queue under Settings → What's New. You approve, edit first, deny, or archive. Only on approval does anything reach the other staff (pop-up, bell, email).

The one missing piece is exactly what you're asking for: **nothing tells you the draft arrived.** You only find it if you happen to open that screen. The unread badge on the What's New menu item counts *published* announcements, not pending ones.

## What this adds

When a new auto-drafted update lands in the queue, you get a notification of your own:

- A bell notification titled **"New update ready to review — <the update's title>"**, with the first lines of the announcement as the body, linking straight to the approval queue.
- It goes to the **owner only**. No other staff member is told anything until you approve.
- A gold count on the **What's New** menu item for pending drafts, so the queue is visible from anywhere in the portal.
- Optional email to you for the same event, controlled by your existing notification preferences — off unless you want it on.

Approving still works exactly as it does now: the staff pop-up, the bell, the email and the read receipts are untouched.

## What stays out

- No notification to staff for a pending draft — ever. The queue is yours alone.
- Hand-written submissions from management already show up in the same queue; they will now notify you too, which is the same gap being closed.
- Nothing is auto-approved and nothing is sent on a timer.
