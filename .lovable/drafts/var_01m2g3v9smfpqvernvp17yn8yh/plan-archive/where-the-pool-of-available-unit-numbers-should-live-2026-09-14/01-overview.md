# Where the pool of available unit numbers should live

Short answer: yes — Vehicle Hub is the right home. It is the one screen that already lists units, and it appears in both the Staff and Management portals, so onboarding staff and management both reach it without a new menu item.

Recommendation: a **Unit Numbers** panel opened from a button in the Vehicle Hub header, not a new sidebar page. Reasons:

- Vehicle Hub is already the place a staff member goes to ask "what unit is that?".
- The pool is a reference list checked occasionally, not a daily worklist — a page in the sidebar would be one more thing to scroll past.
- Opening it as a panel keeps the roster underneath, so staff can glance at the pool and go straight back to the truck they were on.

The picker inside onboarding stays exactly as it is. This panel is for looking, not for assigning.

## What the panel shows

Three short sections, in the same order the picker offers them:

- **Recycled** — freed by a driver who never reached Go-Live, oldest freed first, with the date it came free.
- **Never issued** — gaps inside the sequence.
- **Next in sequence** — one number, today 272.

Plus a one-line summary at the top: how many numbers are free, and what the next one is. A small search box lets staff type a number and see who holds it and whether that driver is live, gone, or still onboarding.
