# Interview notes and three screening columns on Applications

## What you asked for, checked against the app

Everything in your request works, with three things worth knowing before we build:

- **MVR and Clearinghouse already exist** on each application (Not Started / Requested / Received). **PSP does not exist anywhere yet** — it needs to be added to the application record, to the Background Verification panel between MVR and Clearinghouse, and to the driver's onboarding record so it carries over after approval.
- **Interview notes do not exist yet** in any form. They need their own record so several interviewers can each leave a stamped entry.
- **339 applications already exist.** 139 of them already have MVR and Clearinghouse marked Received. Adding PSP as a required check means every one of those shows PSP as "Not Started". Already-approved applications will not be re-blocked — the requirement only applies going forward, at the moment someone tries to approve.

## Recommendations you asked for

**Interview notes — a log of separate entries, each stamped with its author, editable by the author or by management.** When an entry is edited we show a quiet "edited Sep 14" marker next to it, so a note that changed after the fact is visible rather than silent. Deleting an entry is management/owner only. This gives you the multi-interviewer log you want without turning the hiring record into something that can be quietly rewritten. Onboarding staff, management, and owner can all add entries.

**Row expansion — expand only the interview notes, not the whole ribbon.** The Applications list is a scanning tool; the review drawer is where the full record lives. A chevron on the right of each row opens a thin panel underneath showing that applicant's interview entries and an "Add note" box. Only one row is open at a time, and it stays open while you type. Expanding the entire ribbon would duplicate the drawer and make the list heavy.

**Columns — the three checks as small chips, notes as a count.** MVR, PSP, and CH as compact colored chips in one column group; the interview column shows a count plus the most recent interviewer's name, so you can tell at a glance who has been interviewed and who has not. Because that is four new things in an already-full row, I recommend adding the existing **Columns** picker to this page (same one used on the Loads and Brokers lists) so each staff member can hide what they don't need, saved to their own account.
