# Verified: only the truly-archived applicants move

I checked the records before anything moves. Of the 163 applicants sitting in Denied today:

- **50** were put there by the pipeline's Archive action. Every one of them carries the
  "[Archived from pipeline]" tag on the reason, and every one has a matching
  "applicant archived" entry in the audit history with the staff member's name. These are
  the only rows the move touches.
- **106** are genuine denials — a real review decision, some with a written reason, some
  without, and none with any archive entry anywhere in their history. They stay in Denied.
- **7** are a grey area worth your eyes (listed below).

Nobody gets moved because their reason was blank, or because of a guess. The move keys off
the Archive action's own tag, not off anything inferred.
