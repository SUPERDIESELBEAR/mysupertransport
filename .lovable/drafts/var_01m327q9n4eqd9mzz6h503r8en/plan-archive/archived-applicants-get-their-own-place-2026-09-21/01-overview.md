# Archived applicants get their own place

Today the Applications page has only four outcomes: Pending, Revisions, Approved, Denied. When staff archive someone from the onboarding pipeline, the app has nowhere else to put them, so it marks their application **Denied** and stamps the note with "[Archived from pipeline]". That is why Michelle Watts shows as denied even though she was only set aside while she works on her truck.

I checked the records: **50 applicants** are sitting in the Denied tab purely because they were archived from the pipeline — Michelle Watts, Dario Hamilton, Ronald Jeter and 47 others. None of them were actually turned down.

## What changes

- A new **Archived** outcome and a new **Archived** tab on the Applications page, next to Denied.
- Archiving from the onboarding pipeline files the applicant as *Archived*, never Denied.
- On the Applications page, staff can **Archive** an applicant directly (set aside, may be hired later) as an alternative to Deny. Archiving sends no email — unlike a denial, which does.
- From the Archived tab, an applicant can be **moved back to Pending** (to pick them up again) or **moved to Denied** if the decision becomes final.
- The 50 existing records that were archived from the pipeline move out of Denied and into Archived, with their reasons kept. The Denied tab is left with genuine denials only.

## What stays the same

Denied still means denied — the denial email, the recorded reason, the review history. Approved, Pending and Revisions are untouched, and nobody's application data is changed beyond its outcome label.
