# Moving Ali from card 212 to card 224

No code change is needed — this is done in the app today, in Operations → Onboard Systems → Fuel Cards.

Since no fuel charges have come in on 212 yet, nothing financial has to be corrected. Do it in this order:

1. Find card **212** in the assigned list and click **Return**.
2. Choose **"Good — Available to Reissue"**, add a short note such as "issued in error, reassigned to 224", and confirm.
   - 212 moves back to **Unassigned Inventory** and can be handed to anyone.
   - Do **not** use **Deactivate** — that archives the card permanently and takes it out of circulation.
3. Find card **224** in Unassigned Inventory and click **Assign**, choosing Ali.

After that, Ali shows 224 in his Equipment view, and 212 sits available for the next driver.
