# Absence Log — reasons and notes per day, editable, kept as history

This is what the Absence Log is built to do, and it is already in this draft.
Dispatch and management pick a day on a driver's calendar — or a stretch of days
with the Range tool — set the status (Home, Not Dispatched, Truck Down), choose a
reason from a drop-down, and type a comment. It is saved against that date and
read back as a dated time log, so past months stay on the record instead of
being overwritten.

The reason drop-down is greyed out right now with the note "Reasons become
available once this update is accepted." That is not a missing feature — the
reason field is a new field on your live records, and a draft cannot change
live records. It is written and waiting; it switches on the moment the update is
accepted.

## Reasons in the drop-down

Truck Down · Home Time · Vacation · Medical · Personal · Waiting On Load ·
No Driver · Other

Plus a free-text comment box on every entry. Choosing **Other** requires a
comment, so a vague entry can't be saved.

## Editing later

Both dispatch and management can correct an entry: open the same day again and
the current status, reason and comment are already filled in — change them and
save. A whole stretch can be corrected in one go with the Range tool. Each
change is stamped with who made it and when, and writes a line to the driver's
dispatch history, so a correction is visible rather than silent.

## The one step left

Accept this update. On accept: the reason field is added, the drop-down and
comment box unlock in both the day popover and the Range tool, and the Absence
Log starts labelling each stretch with its reason. The calendar colours you have
back now are unaffected.
