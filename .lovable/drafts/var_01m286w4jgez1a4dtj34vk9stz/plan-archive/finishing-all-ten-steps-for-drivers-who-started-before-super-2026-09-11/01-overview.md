# Finishing all ten steps for drivers who started before SUPERDRIVE

Cortez Nelson is the example. He has a real signed agreement, a real ELD, dash camera, fuel card and a Missouri plate — but none of it was created inside SUPERDRIVE. Checked against his live record: no agreement on file (which is why "View Executed ICA" says none found), no assignment sheet, three pieces of equipment still open and one plate still assigned.

So three of the ten offboarding steps have nothing to work with and skip themselves: **4 Lease Termination**, **5 Equipment Return**, and **8 ICA Void**. He is not alone — 20 active drivers have no agreement recorded, and 50 have no assignment sheet.

This update lets every driver finish all ten steps, by letting staff record what already exists on paper.

## What changes

**Record the old agreement.** A short "Record existing agreement" form, available both on the driver's ICA section and directly inside the wizard's Lease Termination step. Truck year, make, model, VIN, plate and owner details are pre-filled from what SUPERDRIVE already holds for that driver; staff confirm or correct them and enter the date the original lease started. Uploading a scan of the signed PDF is encouraged but optional — when there is no copy, the record is saved and clearly marked "no signed copy on file."

Once recorded, the agreement shows as an executed agreement of record, and steps 4 and 8 work exactly as they do for everyone else: sign Appendix C, preview it, email insurance, then mark the agreement void with a reason. Same document, same insurance email, nothing special-cased.

**Equipment return without an assignment sheet.** The return step gathers what the driver actually holds today — his ELD, dash camera, fuel card, BestPass, and Missouri plate — shows the list with serial numbers for staff to confirm or uncheck, and then sends the same return instructions email. Confirming creates a return-only record behind the scenes, so the return is tracked, receipts can be uploaded, and the step completes normally.

**Nothing is invented.** Every value is pulled from existing records and shown for confirmation. If a serial is missing, the line says so rather than guessing.

## What does not change

The ten steps and their order, the typed-name confirmation, the insurance email, void-not-delete on the agreement, and the rule that the wizard is the only place to start offboarding.
