# Archived applicants need their tab back

Answering your questions from the live system:

- **Archiving from the pipeline no longer files anyone as Denied.** The pipeline writes the outcome
  "archived" (confirmed in the pipeline screen's archive action).
- **The archived outcome exists in the data.** Counting applications today: 113 denied, 96 approved,
  78 pending, **50 archived**, 7 revisions requested. Those 50 are the ones previously archived from
  the pipeline.
- **But the Applications page has no Archived tab.** Its tab row is Pending, Revisions, Approved,
  Denied, All, Invited. So the 50 archived applicants show up only under "All" — there is no way to
  see just them, and no Archive button on the page itself.
- The review drawer already understands archived: it styles it neutrally, shows a "set aside"
  reason panel, and offers "move back to Pending" / "Deny instead" — but the page never passes it
  those actions, so they never appear.

So yes: archiving is treated as different from denying, the outcome is real, and the missing piece
is the Archived section on the Applications page.
