# Deactivating a driver: one path, saved as you go

## Where to begin

Open the driver from **Drivers**, then press **Deactivate & Delease** on their profile. That is the only correct starting point. It opens the full wizard on its own screen. Do not start from the plate hub, the fuel card hub, the equipment hub, or the lease terminations list — those are for looking things up, not for offboarding.

Owner and management only.

## The ten steps, in order

1. **Reason & date** — why they are leaving and the last day. You type the driver's name to confirm; if they still look dispatched, you get a warning and the option to park them instead.
2. **Unit disposition** — does the truck leave with the driver, stay leased to us, or undecided.
3. **DOT consultant notice** — sends the notification and stamps the date.
4. **Lease termination (Appendix C)** — sign it, read it, send it to insurance from the same step.
5. **Equipment return** — assignment sheet, return instructions email, decal removal photos, shipping receipt.
6. **Fuel card** — deactivate any assigned card.
7. **Plate release** — release the Missouri plate, or keep it with the unit if the truck stays.
8. **End the agreement** — mark the ICA void with a reason. The record stays, stamped VOID.
9. **Login** — keep their sign-in or switch it off.
10. **Confirm & finish** — review everything done and skipped, then finalize.

Any step can be skipped with a written reason. Nothing is required to be done in one sitting.

## The fix this needs

Today the wizard only records your run **when you press Finish**. Stop halfway and the app remembers nothing about it — even though the letters really went out. That is exactly what happened with Trovino, Bilal and Willie.

This plan changes it so **each step saves the moment you complete or skip it**. The driver's profile then shows "Offboarding in progress — 6 of 10 done" with a **Resume** button that drops you at the first unfinished step.
